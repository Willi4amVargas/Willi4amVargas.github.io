## Overview
A comprehensive, open-source web platform for cataloging Venezuelan films and related news, enabling registered users to contribute and manage movie and director data.

## Key Features & Contribution
- **User-Driven Content**: Registered users can submit and manage new movie and director entries, fostering a community-maintained database.
- **Integrated News Feed**: A dedicated section provides up-to-date news and articles relevant to Venezuelan cinema.
- **Director Registry**: Detailed view of registered directors, linking them directly to their filmography on the platform.
