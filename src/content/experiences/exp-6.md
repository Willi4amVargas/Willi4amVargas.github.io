## Overview
Full frontend React application for the "Latitud 58" fast-food restaurant, implementing a user-friendly interface for online ordering with direct integration to DoorDash and a custom Google API-powered contact form.

## Key Features & Impact
- **Seamless Online Ordering**: Implemented a direct, highly visible link and call-to-action for DoorDash, significantly streamlining the process for customers to place delivery orders.
- **Google API-Powered Contact Form**: Developed and integrated a custom contact form that utilizes the Google API to send emails directly, ensuring reliable and immediate communication between the customer and the restaurant.
- **Responsive and Modern Frontend**: Built the entire application with React and Tailwind CSS, guaranteeing a fast, modern, and fully responsive user experience across all devices.
